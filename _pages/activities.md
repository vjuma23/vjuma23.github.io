## Academic service and organization

I contribute to academic community-building through seminar organization, conference activities, research mentoring, and training in computational mathematics.

### Selected activities

- Co-organizer, UBC Mathematical Biology Seminar.
- Organizing committee member, Frontiers in Biophysics, University of British Columbia.
- Instructor/tutor, 3MC–PIMS–IDMS–ICMS Summer School in Quantitative Molecular and Cellular Biology, University of Manitoba, 2025.
- FEniCSx tutorial instruction for students at the University of Malawi, 2025.
- Poster presenter, Society for Mathematical Biology Annual Meeting, Alberta, 2025.

I am also interested in collaborative projects that connect mathematical analysis, computation, and experimentally motivated biological questions.
